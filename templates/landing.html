How ParkShare works

Two sides, one booking. Pick the side that fits you.

1

Search

Type an area and filter by vehicle type, price, CCTV, covered parking, EV charging and more.

2

Choose your time

Pick vehicle type, start time and how long. You see straight away whether a bay is free for that window.

3

Pay with Razorpay

Your booking is confirmed after payment, with a 6-character code and a QR to show at the gate.

4

Park and leave

If you stay longer than booked, you pay only for the extra time when you check out.

1

Create your listing

Add photos, a Google Maps link, the vehicle types you accept and how many bays each has.

2

Set your rules

Choose slot prices, overtime charges such as ₹6 a minute or ₹100 per 30 minutes, opening hours and cancellation policy.

3

Go live

Once approved, drivers can find and book your parking. Full bays show as booked automatically.

4

Verify at the gate

Enter the driver's code at entry and again at exit. Track earnings, reviews and driver history.

Built around how parking really works

Availability, overtime and trust are handled by the platform, so neither side argues at the gate.

Availability per vehicle type and time

A lot can be full at 2 PM and free at 6 PM. ParkShare checks capacity for your exact window, per vehicle type, so bays are never double-booked.

Car8 of 12 free
Bike21 of 30 free
Truck1 of 2 free

Overtime billed by the owner's own rate

Owners set the price for extra time, for example ₹6 per minute or ₹100 per 30 minutes. At checkout the driver sees the exact amount and pays it online, so nothing is settled in cash arguments.

45 min over at ₹100 / 30 min = ₹200

Code check-in and check-out

The owner confirms the code at entry and exit, so every stay has a record.

Verified badge

Listings reviewed by the ParkShare team carry a badge drivers can trust.

Filters that matter

CCTV, covered, guard, EV charging, 24x7 and cancellation options, all searchable.

Ratings both ways

Drivers rate parkings. Owners record satisfied or not, which feeds a driver's points.

Refunds through Razorpay

Where a parking allows cancellation, refunds go back to the original payment method.

Owner analytics

Earnings, occupancy and peak hours by day, month or year in live charts.
