1. What ParkShare is

ParkShare is a marketplace that connects drivers who need parking with owners who offer it. ParkShare provides the booking, payment and check-in tools. The parking space itself is provided by the owner, who is responsible for it.

2. Accounts

You must give accurate details and keep your password private. You choose whether to register as a driver or an owner. We may disable accounts that break these terms, commit fraud, or put others at risk.

3. Bookings and payment

  • Choosing a time and paying creates a booking. A bay is held for 10 minutes while you pay. If payment is not completed, the hold ends.
  • Prices are set by owners and shown before you pay. Payments are processed by Razorpay; ParkShare does not store your card or UPI details.
  • After payment you receive a 6-character code and QR. Show it to the owner at entry and again at exit.
  • You must check in within the owner's grace period after your start time. Otherwise the booking is a no-show, is not refunded, and affects your driver points.

4. Staying longer than booked

If you stay past your end time you pay the owner's overtime rate shown on the parking page, rounded up to the next block, after any free minutes. You pay this through ParkShare when the owner checks you out.

5. Cancellations and refunds

Each owner decides whether a parking allows cancellation, how far ahead you must cancel, and whether a cancellation charge applies. Where cancellation is allowed you can request a refund before the cancel window closes; the owner approves or declines it. If an owner cancels your booking you receive a full refund. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through Razorpay and may take several working days to appear. Overtime payments are not refunded automatically.

6. Driver points, reviews and conduct

Drivers start with 100 points. Points go up or down according to the platform's settings (for example completed stays, no-shows and unverified checkouts). Owners can see a driver's points and history when that driver has booked with them. Reviews must be honest and respectful; we may remove content that is abusive or unlawful.

7. Owners

Owners must list only parking they are entitled to offer, describe it accurately (location, prices, hours, rules), keep it safe, and honour confirmed bookings. A listing may need approval before it is shown, and we may suspend listings that are inaccurate or unsafe. Platform fees, if any, are shown in your earnings. Payments are collected by ParkShare and paid out to owners; your balance is visible in your account.

8. Liability

You park at your own risk. Owners are responsible for their premises. To the extent the law allows, ParkShare is not liable for loss of or damage to vehicles or belongings, or for disputes between drivers and owners, though we will help where we can.

9. Changes and contact

We may update these terms; continuing to use ParkShare means you accept the updated version. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
